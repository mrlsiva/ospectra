# Built for Care at Home

## One Operating Layer for Home Care and Home Health

Care at home depends on constant coordination between referrals, authorizations, clinicians, caregivers, schedules, documentation, payers, and families. OSpectra connects those moving parts so approved care becomes a prepared worker, a completed visit, a defensible record, and a clean claim.

## Home Care

### Protect Every Authorized Hour

Coordinate caregiver availability, credentials, client preferences, continuity, schedules, EVV, and payroll—while there is still time to protect the visit.

## Home Health

### Keep Every Episode Moving

Connect referrals, orders, clinician readiness, visit plans, documentation, and claim requirements so care does not stall between teams and systems.

## From Referral to Reimbursement

### Start Care Faster

Identify missing authorizations, orders, assessments, credentials, and intake information before they delay the first visit.

### Protect Every Visit

Surface coverage risks, readiness gaps, and backup options before scheduled care becomes missed care.

### Prove What Happened

Keep EVV, visit notes, signatures, tasks, and supporting evidence aligned while the work is still fresh.

### Move Cleanly to Payment

Connect authorized service, delivered care, documentation, claims, and payroll so teams can resolve exceptions without rebuilding the visit.

## Works With the Systems You Already Use

OSpectra connects scheduling, EVV, EHR, ATS, payer, and payroll systems—creating one operational view without forcing agencies to replace their existing technology.

> The problem is rarely one broken system. It is the space between systems, teams, and decisions. OSpectra makes that space visible and actionable.

**AI handles the coordination. People remain in charge of care.**
