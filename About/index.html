ABOUT US

AI Coworkers for the People
Who Deliver Care

OSpectra builds AI coworkers that help Home Care and Home Health agencies coordinate the work surrounding care. We connect client engagement, workforce readiness, service delivery, and financial operations, helping teams move from the first inquiry through completed visits and payment.

Our platform brings 14 specialized coworkers together with shared context, connected systems, and human oversight. Agencies can coordinate work across their existing tools while keeping people in charge of care and consequential decisions.

Our mission is to make agency operations easier to manage, giving teams more time to support the people who depend on them.
