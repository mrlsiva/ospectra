WHAT THIS AGENT OWNS

The connective layer across the whole agency.

Command Center does not replace the specialized agents — it combines, ranks, and explains what they already know.

01
Unified operational view

Combines client demand, workforce capacity, coverage, provider readiness, visit delivery, authorization, and revenue into one view.

02
Signal prioritization

Ranks issues by urgency, client impact, service risk, compliance exposure, financial impact, and time remaining to act.

03
Root-cause chains

Explains the upstream cause and downstream consequence of each issue so teams solve the chain, not just the symptom.

04
Cross-functional actions

Recommends actions across teams and assigns clear ownership without absorbing each agent's specialized workflow.

05
Multi-dimensional views

Lets owners view performance by location, program, payer, role, client, or provider from one source of context.

06
Growth & capacity scenarios

Translates demand, workforce, service, and financial signals into scenarios with the Data Analyst and Scientist Agent.

07
Feedback & policy learning

Tracks approvals, overrides, outcomes, and patterns so leadership can improve the rule behind recurring issues.

HOW IT WORKS

Signals in, a decision out.

Three connected steps carry raw operational signal into a ranked, owned, and traceable decision.

1

STEP ONESense

Combine permitted operational signals from the specialized agents into one current agency picture.

2

STEP TWOPrioritize

Rank the decisions that matter by care impact, urgency, compliance, financial exposure, and time to act.

3

STEP THREECoordinate and learn

Assign ownership, track outcomes, and improve the policy or workflow that produced the issue.

CONNECTED HANDOFF

Command Center connects the specialized agents without absorbing them. The independent Data Analyst and Scientist Agent supplies explainable analysis; Command Center places that guidance beside operational priorities and ownership.

BUILT-IN PRINCIPLE

One shared care graph, role-based access, evidence attached to every recommendation, and human approval for consequential action.
