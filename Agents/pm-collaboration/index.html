● VERSION 2 / 09 / PM (COLLABORATION, COORDINATION, AND COMMUNICATION) AGENT

Coordinate every internal team through the channel that works for them.

A project manager can brief OSpectra once and let it conduct the internal outreach. The agent reaches each team member through a preferred channel — including WhatsApp, automated phone conversations, email, or another configured option — then organizes the replies and returns only the action items that require the PM's opinion.

WHAT THIS AGENT OWNS

Every team member, reached the way they actually respond.

A living directory of who to reach, how, and when — then the follow-through to make sure nothing sits unanswered.

  • PR
    Team profiles

    Maintain an internal communication profile for each team member: preferred channel and time, timezone, language, availability, and escalation preferences.

  • OU
    Outreach execution

    Carry out approved outreach through WhatsApp, email, automated phone conversations, or another configured internal channel.

  • RT
    Response tracking

    Monitor responses, follow up with non-responders, consolidate answers, and summarize conflicting input or unresolved questions.

  • SC
    Scheduling & tasks

    Schedule meetings, coordinate availability, create and assign tasks, set deadlines, send reminders, and update project status.

  • ES
    Risk & escalation

    Identify blockers, unreachable people, missed commitments, and emerging delivery risk, then escalate at the appropriate threshold.

  • PM
    Project memory

    Preserve project memory across messages, call records and transcripts, contact attempts, decisions, commitments, task changes, and unresolved issues.

HOW IT WORKS

One brief, sent, delivered, and read.

Three steps carry a single instruction from the PM into completed coordination across the whole team.

✓ Sent

STEP ONEBrief

The PM states the objective, people, context, timing, and any boundaries once.

✓✓ Delivered

STEP TWOReach

The agent coordinates autonomously through each person's preferred internal channel and maintains follow-up.

✓✓ Read

STEP THREEReturn decisions

The agent completes routine coordination and brings the PM context, options, and a recommendation wherever judgment is required.

WHAT THE TEAM SEES

A channel list, not a stack of separate reports.

Everything the PM needs sits in one sidebar — scannable the way any messaging app is.

CHANNELS
  • Outreach status

    Who was contacted, by which channel, response state, next attempt, and escalation point.

    Live
  • Consolidated responses

    A clear summary of agreements, conflicts, open questions, and people still outstanding.

    2 open
  • Tasks and blockers

    Owners, deadlines, commitments, dependencies, risks, and items requiring the PM's opinion.

    5 items
  • Project memory

    A durable record of communication, calls, decisions, actions, changes, and unresolved issues.

    Synced
CONNECTED HANDOFF

The PM Agent is for employees and internal teams only. It links project-level status and escalation signals to Command Center while remaining a separate collaboration and coordination function.

BUILT-IN PRINCIPLE

Routine internal coordination is autonomous. Every action item that requires the PM's opinion returns with context, options, and a recommendation before the agent proceeds.
