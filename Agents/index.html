THE CONNECTED AGENT TEAM

One operating graph. Fourteen specialized agents.

Each agent owns a clear outcome while sharing the client, provider, visit, project, growth, policy, and evidence context it is permitted to use. The result is a connected operating system with role-specific intelligence — not a collection of disconnected copilots.

01

Marketing, SDR, Customer Engagement, and Customer Success attract, convert, onboard, and retain the right relationships.

02

03

Governance and Economics

04

Intelligence and Leadership

Data Analyst & Scientist and Command Center turn connected signals into explainable guidance and coordinated action.

DESIGN RATIONALE

Agent boundaries reflected in this draft.

  • Scheduling and provider matching operate as one agent because coverage decisions depend on both availability and fit.
  • Credentialing, compliance, visit verification, documentation quality, and provider performance sit inside Provider Success.
  • Authorization monitoring and payer readiness sit inside Billing, alongside claim preparation and exception resolution.
  • Demand forecasting informs Recruitment and Scheduling rather than becoming a separate agent.
  • The agency-wide operations layer is named the Command Center Agent.
  • Customer Success owns the ongoing client experience after service begins; Customer Engagement owns inquiry, intake, referral relationships, and meeting coordination.
  • Payroll, Compliance and Audit, Training, PM, Data Analyst and Scientist, SDR, and Marketing remain standalone agents with explicit handoffs to the rest of the portfolio.
  • SDR and Marketing remain separate: SDR owns website conversion, visitor intent, and initial outreach; Marketing owns audience intelligence, campaign creation, and ongoing optimization.
SHARED CONTROL MODEL

Agents recommend and prepare actions; agency staff approve consequential decisions, inspect the evidence, and change the governing rule.
