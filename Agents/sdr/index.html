Responsibilities

What this agent owns

Framed the way a website builder's settings panel would present it — every row is a lever this agent is allowed to pull.

Demand Generation · Settings Active
  • 01 Assess the website, positioning, messages, user journey, and outreach methods to identify changes that could strengthen demand generation.
  • 02 Add or recommend interactive calls to action, forms, offers, chat, landing experiences, and other conversion paths for a static site.
  • 03 Track anonymous and identified visitor or company behavior when legally and technically permitted, including source, path, engagement, intent, and conversion.
  • 04 Test headlines, copy, calls to action, forms, layouts, offers, images, chat, and outreach inside the owner's approved guardrails.
  • 05 Identify buying intent, conduct the initial qualification and outreach, and prioritize the leads most likely to benefit from a live conversation.
  • 06 Report experiment results and request owner approval when a proposed change falls outside the approved strategy, brand, channel, or operating boundary.
