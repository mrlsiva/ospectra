How it works

Three stages, one narrowing pipeline

Every candidate that reaches onboarding has moved through the same funnel — wide at the top where demand is forecast, narrow at the bottom where only qualified, ready people remain.

Responsibilities

What this agent owns

01 Forecast hiring needs by role, geography, shift, program, and service demand using signals from intake and scheduling.
02 Screen candidates for credentials, availability, transportation, location, experience, and care-specific fit.
03 Rank candidates with clear fit evidence and show which open or forecasted needs each person could cover.
04 Coordinate interviews, reminders, follow-ups, and clean handoffs to the responsible recruiter or hiring manager.
05 Rediscover qualified candidates and keep relevant backups warm before purchasing additional applicant volume.
06 Track the pipeline from applicant through qualified, interviewing, offer-ready, and onboarding-ready stages.
