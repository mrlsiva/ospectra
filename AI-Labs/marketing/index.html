WHAT THIS AGENT OWNS

A creative brief that runs itself.

Six responsibilities, from raw audience evidence to finished, budget-aware campaigns.

01

Audience intelligence. Analyze visitor source, geography, demographics, behavior, engagement, intent, and downstream outcomes using legally permitted data from SDR and Customer Engagement.

02

ICP & segments. Create and refine ideal customer profiles and audience segments based on evidence rather than assumptions alone.

03

Channel strategy. Choose the best approved platform, timing, cadence, message, content type, offer, and creative direction for each audience.

04

Creative production. Produce finished campaign assets across connected channels, including copy, graphics, video, advertisements, email, social content, and landing pages.

05

Autonomous optimization. After initial approval, autonomously optimize creative, audience, timing, channel mix, and budget allocation through bounded A/B testing.

06

Budget governance. Stay within the owner's total budget; when additional spend could help, request approval with the expected benefit, reasoning, data, assumptions, and tradeoffs.

WHAT THE TEAM SEES

Creative and analytical, side by side.

Four purpose-built views — who to reach, what to ship, what worked, and what to spend next.

ICP intelligence

Who is responding, where they come from, what they care about, and how the segment is changing.

Campaign studio

The channel plan, schedule, message system, creative assets, approvals, and launch readiness.

Performance attribution

Which audiences, channels, content, and variations create qualified leads, meetings, and conversions.

Budget recommendations

Current spend, expected return, remaining room, and reasoned requests for any increase.

CONNECTED HANDOFF

Marketing receives visitor behavior from SDR and downstream outcomes from SDR and Customer Engagement. Those results continuously refine the ICP, targeting, timing, messages, creative, and channel mix.

BUILT-IN PRINCIPLE

The owner approves the initial strategy and sets the budget. The agent then optimizes within those limits and requests permission, with evidence, before expanding them.
