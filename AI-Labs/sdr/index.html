Responsibilities

What this agent owns

Framed the way a website builder's settings panel would present it — every row is a lever this agent is allowed to pull.

01

Assess the website, positioning, messages, user journey, and outreach methods to identify changes that could strengthen demand generation.

02

Add or recommend interactive calls to action, forms, offers, chat, landing experiences, and other conversion paths for a static site.

03

Track anonymous and identified visitor or company behavior when legally and technically permitted, including source, path, engagement, intent, and conversion.

04

Test headlines, copy, calls to action, forms, layouts, offers, images, chat, and outreach inside the owner's approved guardrails.

05

Identify buying intent, conduct the initial qualification and outreach, and prioritize the leads most likely to benefit from a live conversation.

06

Report experiment results and request owner approval when a proposed change falls outside the approved strategy, brand, channel, or operating boundary.

Connected handoff

SDR owns website conversion, visitor intent, qualification, and initial outreach. Customer Engagement owns the live conversation and meeting booking; Marketing receives visitor data whether the lead is won, lost, or disengaged.

Built-in principle

Approval first, then autonomy within clear guardrails. Visitor identification, tracking, consent, outreach, cookies, opt-outs, and do-not-contact rules stay within applicable legal limits.
