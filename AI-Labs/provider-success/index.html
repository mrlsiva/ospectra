How it works

One provider journey, three connected stages

Every provider follows the same road — from readiness checks before a visit is ever assigned, through respectful field support, to verification and fair, evidence-backed improvement.

01 Prepare 02 Support 03 Verify & improve
01

Prepare

Confirm compliance and visit readiness, then deliver the context the provider needs before arrival.

  • Track credentials, training, required documents, expirations, and program-specific compliance so readiness gaps surface before assignment or service.
  • Coordinate care-plan changes, clarify approved tasks, and deliver a concise pre-visit brief with the schedule, client context, risks, and updates.
02

Support

Coordinate care-plan updates, guide completion, and recommend next steps when a visit or field situation is at risk.

  • Guide visit completion with timely prompts that support the provider without creating unnecessary administrative burden.
  • When an emergency, safety concern, or field issue appears, summarize the evidence and recommend the necessary next steps to authorized staff without independently making clinical or emergency decisions.
03

Verify & improve

Resolve evidence and documentation gaps, then turn patterns into fair, actionable support.

  • Verify the delivered visit against the schedule, EVV timestamps, location evidence, required tasks, and available care documentation.
  • Check notes for completeness, internal consistency, care-plan alignment, and downstream billing readiness; request only the specific correction needed.
  • Surface evidence-backed performance insights and recommend coaching, recognition, follow-up, or workload adjustments while keeping employment decisions with agency leadership.

Connected handoff

Provider Success receives confirmed assignments from Scheduling and supplies Billing with verified visit evidence and Payroll with timesheets. Material provider and field risks also surface in Compliance and Command Center.

Built-in principle

Field-first support. The agent prepares evidence and recommended next steps; authorized people retain clinical, emergency, employment, and other consequential decisions.
