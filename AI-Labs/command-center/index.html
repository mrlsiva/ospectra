WHAT THIS COWORKER OWNS

The connective layer across the whole agency.

Command Center does not replace the specialized coworkers — it combines, ranks, and explains what they already know.

01

Unified operational view. Combines client demand, workforce capacity, coverage, provider readiness, visit delivery, authorization, and revenue into one view.

02

Signal prioritization. Ranks issues by urgency, client impact, service risk, compliance exposure, financial impact, and time remaining to act.

03

Root-cause chains. Explains the upstream cause and downstream consequence of each issue so teams solve the chain, not just the symptom.

04

Cross-functional actions. Recommends actions across teams and assigns clear ownership without absorbing each coworker's specialized workflow.

05

Multi-dimensional views. Lets owners view performance by location, program, payer, role, client, or provider from one source of context.

06

Growth & capacity scenarios. Translates demand, workforce, service, and financial signals into scenarios with the Data Analyst and Scientist Coworker.

07

Feedback & policy learning. Tracks approvals, overrides, outcomes, and patterns so leadership can improve the rule behind recurring issues.
