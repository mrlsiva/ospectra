WHAT THIS AGENT OWNS

Compliance and audit duties.

01

Compliance calendar & obligations. Maintain the agency compliance calendar, policy obligations, required reviews, evidence requests, and accountable owners.

02

Incident & risk intake. Receive incidents and risk signals, classify them using configured rules, preserve the original report, and identify required notifications or escalation.

03

Investigation coordination. Coordinate investigation tasks, supporting evidence, interviews, findings, and follow-up without substituting for authorized judgment.

04

Corrective & preventive actions. Create and monitor corrective and preventive action plans with owners, due dates, validation criteria, and closure evidence.

05

Audit-ready evidence packets. Prepare audit-ready evidence packets that connect the requirement, policy, event, decision, approval, and proof of completion.

06

Pattern & exposure analysis. Analyze recurring incidents, gaps, overdue actions, and policy failures so leaders can reduce future exposure.

WHAT THE TEAM SEES

Four views, one record of record.

Compliance calendar

Upcoming obligations, renewals, reviews, submissions, and owners.

Incident and risk worklist

Open events ranked by severity, required action, exposure, and reporting deadline.

Corrective actions

Findings, owners, due dates, completion evidence, and effectiveness checks.

Audit readiness

Evidence coverage by requirement, with missing or inconsistent proof clearly identified.

Connected Handoff

Provider Success supplies relevant field and provider signals while retaining provider readiness. Compliance and Audit coordinates agency-level response and returns required actions to the responsible agent and Command Center.

Built-in Principle

Evidence first, human authority intact. The agent prepares and coordinates; designated leaders retain legal, regulatory, employment, and reporting decisions.
