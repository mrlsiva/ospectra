Scheduling & Provider Matching Agent

Fill every visit with the right provider — not simply the next available one.

OSpectra combines scheduling and provider matching because a filled slot is only successful when the assigned provider is qualified, ready, available, and appropriate for the client. Every recommendation connects coverage pressure with evidence of fit.

What this agent owns

Coverage requirements, ranked and matched.

01

Translate each approved service plan into concrete coverage requirements, including timing, location, continuity, skills, credentials, and client-specific considerations.

02

Rank qualified providers using availability, distance, transportation, experience, credential status, client preferences, and skill or behavior-level fit.

03

Detect uncovered visits, recurring capacity gaps, risky schedules, and future shortages before they become day-of-service emergencies.

04

Recommend the best assignment, alternate options, and qualified backups with the reason for each ranking visible to the scheduler.

05

Coordinate confirmations, changes, cancellations, and reassignment workflows without losing the history behind the decision.

06

Send unresolved coverage demand to Recruitment and confirmed assignments to Provider Success for readiness preparation.

Connected handoff

Scheduling & Provider Matching receives approved service requests from Customer Engagement and available candidates from Recruitment. Confirmed assignments move to Provider Success for readiness preparation; unresolved coverage demand and systemic risk roll up to Recruitment and Command Center.

Built-in principle

Fit before fill. Recommendations are explainable, scheduling decisions remain human-controlled, and client continuity is treated as an operating constraint.
