How it works

An ongoing rhythm of check-ins

Every relationship follows the same continuing cadence — listen, coordinate, and strengthen — so nothing depends on someone remembering to follow up.

01

Listen

Maintain the agreed check-in rhythm and capture what the client or family is experiencing.

02

Coordinate

Route each need to the right owner and keep the relationship updated until the loop is closed.

03

Strengthen

Turn resolved concerns and positive moments into retention, trust, and appropriate advocacy.

Scope

What this agent owns

  • ✓ Schedule proactive check-ins with clients and families based on service stage, preferences, prior concerns, and relationship health.
  • ✓ Capture satisfaction, changing preferences, service requests, compliments, concerns, and commitments in one continuing relationship record.
  • ✓ Coordinate non-clinical service changes with the responsible operational agent while keeping the client informed of progress.
  • ✓ Detect early signs of dissatisfaction, disengagement, or unmet expectations and recommend a recovery plan before the relationship is lost.
  • ✓ Prepare follow-up messages, resolution summaries, renewal conversations, and advocacy requests for the appropriate human owner.
  • ✓ Track recurring experience patterns so leadership can improve service, communication, and retention across the agency.
Visibility

What the team sees

Health

Client health

Relationship status, recent feedback, open commitments, and the next recommended check-in.

Concerns

Open concerns

Issues organized by urgency, owner, promised response, and time remaining to resolve.

Changes

Service changes

Requested preference or service updates and the downstream workflow responsible for each one.

Retention

Recovery and retention

At-risk relationships, suggested recovery plans, and advocacy opportunities after successful service.

Connected handoff

Customer Engagement hands off the relationship when service begins. Customer Success then works with Provider Success, Scheduling, Billing, and Command Center to resolve needs without absorbing those workflows.

Built-in principle

Proactive and human. The agent keeps the relationship visible, while sensitive conversations and consequential service decisions remain with authorized staff.
