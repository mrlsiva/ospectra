● Why it looks different

Built to think, not to follow a script.

Real conversations don’t follow a diagram — so our agents don’t either. Every agent runs on a reasoning core that thinks through each situation and decides its own next step, inside limits the platform itself enforces.

Fewer boxes. Far fewer dead ends. Agents that finish the job, not the script.

Every layer of the platform is measured before it ships and enforced by the runtime itself — not by instructions we hope the AI follows. Everything on this page is here because it earned its place.

● Inside the Agent Runtime

Twelve engines. One promise: work you can trust.

Every agent — ours or the ones you author — runs inside the same engines. A capability proven once is proven for all of them.

01

Reasoning Engine

Thinks through real work end to end — plans, acts, and recovers from dead ends. Agents that finish jobs, not tickets.

02

Context Engine

Every conversation starts fully briefed: who this is, what they told you before, what the moment needs. Your customers never repeat themselves.

03

Memory Engine

Total recall, on the record. What was said six weeks ago is still there — in full — the moment it matters.

04

Guardrails Engine

Brand-safe by construction. Protections for data, conduct and manipulation that can be tightened to your policies — and never loosened below ours.

05

Approval Gates

The moments that matter wait for your people — money, signatures, saying no to a customer. Frozen until a human rules, even when the agent runs overnight.

a person decides here
06

The Validator

Independent quality control on every piece of work. A second mind — that never sees the first one’s reasoning — confirms the job is actually done before anyone hears “done.”

07

Evaluation Engine

Proof before production. Every change faces a bank of real-world scenarios. Nothing ships on the strength of a good demo.

08

Learning Engine

Gets better at your business. A thumbs-up becomes a worked example; a correction becomes a rule — with your team approving every lesson.

09

Usage & Cost Engine

A CFO-grade meter. Live spend per agent and per customer, priced as it happens. A retry never bills twice.

10

OpenSandbox

Anything an agent executes happens in a sealed room — an isolated container per conversation, away from your systems.

11

MCP Pack Host

Your systems, plugged in. CRM, records, scheduling — delivered as packs that go live in the running platform in about thirty seconds, without downtime.

12

Agent Studio

Build agents in a browser — roles, rules and channels authored by the people who know the work. Every change versioned, every agent with its own changelog.

● Control

Autonomy is a dial, not a leap.

Start with answers only. Grow into unattended operation as the evidence earns it. The platform holds whichever setting you choose.

  • Ask

    Answers questions. Takes no action at all.

  • Plan

    Proposes the steps and stops. Nothing moves until you approve.

  • Agent

    Does the work, pausing at every gated action for a human decision.

    gated actions wait

  • Autonomous

    Runs unattended — and still freezes at the irreversible moments. No setting removes that.

    gated actions wait

The runtime keeps the promise, not the prompt.

Anyone can write “never do X” in an instruction and hope. In a business where an agent talks to real customers, hoping isn’t a control — so the limits live in the platform itself, where no clever conversation can talk its way past them.
